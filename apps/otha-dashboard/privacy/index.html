Privacy Policy

OTHA Dashboard · Effective October 27, 2026

OTHA Dashboard is an internal tool operated by Old Town Hall Associates, LLC ("OTHA"), 1202 Kirkwood Highway, Wilmington, Delaware. This policy explains what it does with data from QuickBooks Online.

Who uses it

Only OTHA's owners and authorized bookkeepers, for companies OTHA owns or manages. It has no public users and no customers.

What it reads

With a company administrator's authorization, it reads accounting data from that company's QuickBooks Online file: accounts, balances, transactions and standard reports. It does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks, and it does not access payment, payroll or banking credentials.

How the data is used

Only to show OTHA's own cash position and financial reports to OTHA's owners and bookkeepers and to check the books at month end. The data is never sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to train machine-learning models for anyone else.

Where it is stored

On computers controlled by OTHA, behind access controls, with connection credentials kept in restricted files that are never published or committed to source control. Copies are kept only as long as needed for OTHA's reporting and records.

Disconnecting

A company administrator can disconnect at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps). After disconnection OTHA Dashboard stops reading data and discards that company's access tokens.

Questions or requests: bookkeeping@othde.com
