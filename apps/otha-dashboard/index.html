OTHA Dashboard

An internal tool of Old Town Hall Associates, LLC

OTHA Dashboard is a private, internal reporting tool used only by Old Town Hall Associates, LLC (OTHA) and the companies it manages. It reads accounting data from those companies' own QuickBooks Online files to show cash balances and run month-end checks for OTHA's owners and bookkeepers.

It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for here. Connecting a company is done by an authorized OTHA administrator.
